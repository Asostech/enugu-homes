A listing site just for Enugu

We are not trying to cover all of Nigeria. The goal is a simple place to find or list houses, flats and land in Enugu State — then talk on WhatsApp.

1. Search an area

GRA, New Haven, Trans-Ekulu, Independence Layout, Abakpa, Uwani, Emene, Ugwuaji, Centenary City.

2. Open a listing

See price in naira, beds, amenities like generator and borehole, then WhatsApp the owner or agent.

3. List for free (launch)

Landlords and agents can add a property from the List page. Replace sample numbers before you share the site publicly.

Always inspect the property. Do not pay inspection or agency fees to a stranger before you meet them and confirm the house exists.
